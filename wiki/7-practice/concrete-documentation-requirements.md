# Concrete drawing and specification documentation checklist

> Scope: minimum items AS 3600 requires to appear on drawings/specification for
> a concrete structure, restated as a review checklist.

## Summary

`[code]` AS 3600:2018 Cl 1.4 lists the minimum information that drawings and/or
specification for a concrete structure must include, when required. Framed as
a checklist:

- [ ] Reference number and date of issue of applicable design Standards.
- [ ] Imposed actions (live loads) used in design.
- [ ] Earthquake design category (from AS 1170.4).
- [ ] Any constraint on construction assumed in the design.
- [ ] Exposure classification for durability (see [[as3600-durability-and-cover]]).
- [ ] Fire resistance level (FRL), if applicable (see [[as3600-fire-resistance-design]]).
- [ ] Class and grade designation of concrete.
- [ ] Any required properties of the concrete.
- [ ] Curing procedure.
- [ ] Grade, Ductility Class and type of reinforcement, and grade/type of tendons.
- [ ] Size, quantity and location of all reinforcement, tendons and structural
      fixings, and the cover to each.
- [ ] Location and details of splices, mechanical connections and welding of
      reinforcement or tendons.
- [ ] Maximum jacking force per tendon and stressing order.
- [ ] Shape and size of each member.
- [ ] Surface finish specified.
- [ ] Minimum period before stripping of forms and removal of shores.
- [ ] Location and details of planned construction/movement joints and their
      protection method.

## Detail

`[code]` This is a documentation completeness check, not a design check — it
governs what must be recorded, not how to calculate it. Use it as a drawing/
spec sign-off gate on a project (see `8-projects` for the project instance) or
as a QA checklist item alongside other `7-practice` review procedures.
