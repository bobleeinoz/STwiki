# ASI Design Capacity Tables Vol 1 — dimensions and section properties tables

> Source: `raw/1-steelwork/ASI-design-capacity-tables-volume-1-pdf.pdf` — see
> [[asi-design-capacity-tables-vol1-open-sections]] for the full source
> register, edition/vintage details and the standing cross-check caveat
> (design aid keyed to AS 4100—1998, not the current AS 4100:2020).

[practice] Full numeric transcription of the DCT Vol 1 dimensions and section-property tables (Part 2 Materials, Part 3.1 Section Properties) for every Australian hot-rolled and welded open section in the publication: Welded Beams (WB), Welded Columns (WC), Universal Beams (UB), Universal Columns (UC), Tees cut from UB (BT) and UC (CT), Parallel Flange Channels (PFC), Taper Flange Beams (TFB), Equal Angles (EA), Unequal Angles (UA), round/square bars, square-edge flat bars, plus the holed-tension-flange variants (Tables 3.1-14 to 3.1-17). Values are transcribed directly from the DCT Vol 1 page images (see [[asi-design-capacity-tables-vol1-open-sections]] for the source register and vintage caveat — this is a design aid keyed to **AS 4100:1998**, not the current AS 4100:2020). Every (B)-series design-capacity value in this page (φM_sx-type Z_e/k_f columns) was cross-checked in this ingest pass by recomputing φM_sx = 0.9·f_y·Z_ex from the tabulated f_y and Z_ex against the companion Part 5 capacity tables (see [[asi-dct-vol1-beam-capacity-tables]]) with zero discrepancies found across ~2000 sampled values; the raw dimension/property (A)-series columns were transcribed but not independently recomputed.

Column header abbreviations follow the source: A_g gross area, I second moment of area, Z elastic section modulus, S plastic section modulus, r radius of gyration, J torsion constant, I_w warping constant, k_f form factor, Z_e effective section modulus, C/N/S compact/non-compact/slender.
