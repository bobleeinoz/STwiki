# AS/NZS 1170.2:2021 — Wind actions

> `raw/0-standards/AS 1170-2-2021-reprint.pdf`. Concept pages live in

> and lattice towers); figures and tables in `0-standards/assets`.

## Summary

`[code]` AS/NZS 1170.2:2021 sets out procedures for determining wind speeds and
the resulting wind actions for structural design, for wind other than
tornadoes (Cl 1.1). It covers buildings and towers up to 200 m high,
structures with unsupported roof spans under 100 m, offshore structures within
30 km of the coast, and other structures except offshore structures beyond
30 km, bridges, wind-farm structures and power transmission/distribution
structures (Cl 1.1(a)-(d)). It is to be read with AS/NZS 1170.0 and may be used
to demonstrate conformance with NCC (Australia) Part B1 (Cl 1.2).

`[code]` It is a **loading** standard; member design checks come from the
material standards ([[as-4100-2020-steel-structures]],
[[as-3600-2018-concrete-structures]]). The 2021 edition is the Australian/New
Zealand joint revision (preface lists changes such as revised Region A0
multipliers, lee multipliers for NZ, new Cl 5.3.4 open area/volume factor K_v,
new dynamic-response methods for poles with headframes and horizontal slender
structures, revised C_fs equations, and new notes for complex porous industrial
plants in Appendix C).

`[derived]` **Relationship to AS 3774.** [[as-3774-1996-loads-on-bulk-solids-containers]]
Cl 7.1 sends wind design to "AS 1170.2" (the 1996-era standard). AS/NZS 1170.2:2021
is the current wind standard in this wiki; its Appendix A.5 gives circular
bin/silo/tank pressure coefficients. Edition currency of the raw copy beyond
the 2021 print was not verified; check Standards Australia before live use.

## Contradictions

None recorded against other wiki pages. Cross-standard note: AS 3774 Cl 7.1.2
quotes a −0.8 wind suction coefficient for unroofed containers; the AS/NZS
1170.2:2021 open-top bin rule is Eq A.5(5) (see
[[as1170-2-bins-silos-and-tanks-wind-pressures]]). Both positions are recorded
on [[as3774-environmental-and-accidental-loads]] and the AS/NZS page.
