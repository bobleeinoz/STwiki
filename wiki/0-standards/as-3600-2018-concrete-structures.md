# AS 3600:2018 — Concrete Structures

> Scope: standard register and section-level clause map for AS 3600:2018. Which
> clause governs what, current edition and amendment status. No clause text
> reproduced — see `raw/0-standards/AS_3600-2018-Reprint.pdf` for the source.

## Summary

`[code]` AS 3600:2018 is the fifth edition of the Australian Standard for the
design and construction of concrete building structures and members containing
reinforcing steel and/or tendons, plus minimum requirements for plain concrete
pedestals and footings. It supersedes AS 3600—2009.

`[code]` This reprint incorporates Amendment No. 1 (November 2018) and
Amendment No. 2 (May 2021). Amended clauses, notes, tables and figures are
marked in the source with a marginal bar and amendment number — check the
marginal bar against any clause cited from this edition before relying on it.

`[code]` Material limits under Clause 1.1.2: concrete characteristic cylinder
strength f'c 20–120 MPa, saturated surface-dry density 1800–2800 kg/m³;
reinforcing steel Ductility Class N or E to AS/NZS 4671 (Class L permitted only
in restricted secondary roles); tendons to AS/NZS 4672.1, tested to
AS/NZS 4672.2; steel fibres to ISO 13270:2013 meeting Clause 16.3.3.8.

## Detail

### Major changes from the 2009 edition

`[code]` Per the Preface, principal differences from AS 3600—2009:

- New Section 14 — Design for Earthquake Actions (previously Appendix C).
- New Section 15 — Diaphragms.
- New Section 16 — Steel Fibre Reinforced Concrete (SFRC).
- New Section 18 — Design for Fatigue.
- New Appendix C — Residual Tensile Strength Test for SFRC.
- Revised: capacity (phi) factors; maximum steel strength; shear in deep
  slabs; fire design (axis distances, continuous top reinforcement, minimum
  slab thickness); shrinkage, creep and deflection models; development
  lengths for higher-strength steels; steel shrinkage in strut-and-tie
  regions; punching shear; ductility of precast connections; heating and
  re-bending of bars; crack control.

Any wiki page that cites an AS 3600 clause touching the items above should
note whether it reflects the 2009 or 2018 provisions — the two editions are
not interchangeable on these points.

### Referenced standards seen in Section 1 scope/definitions

`[code]` Cross-references worth registering separately as they recur across
categories:

- AS/NZS 4671 — reinforcing steel, ductility classes L/N/E.
- AS/NZS 4672.1 / .2 — prestressing tendons, material and test methods.
- AS 1170.4 — earthquake design category (feeds AS 3600 Section 14).
- ISO 13270:2013 — steel fibres for SFRC.
- AS 1530.4 — fire test method underlying FRP definitions in Section 5.
- AS 4997 — maritime structures, noted as out of primary scope for AS 3600.
