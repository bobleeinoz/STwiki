# AS 3774—1996 — Loads on bulk solids containers

> Scope: register and clause map for AS 3774—1996 (second edition,

## Summary

`[code]` AS 3774—1996 gives nationally unified rules for determining loads for
the design of containment structures — bins, silos, bunkers and dump hoppers —
for mass storage of **granular** bulk solids. It does not apply to silage
containers or containers with parameters outside its Section 2 (Cl 1.1).
Prepared by Committee BD/65; approved 30 August 1996, published 5 October
1996; originated as AS 3774—1990; based on the IEAust *Guidelines for the
assessment of loads on bulk solids containers* (1986). The second edition
incorporated an amendment responding to the 1993 AS 1170.4 and to user
comments (principally Sections 4, 6 and 7); the Commentary is AS 3774
Supplement 1 (not ingested). It is a **loading** standard: it defines actions
on the container and supports, not the member design checks, which come from
the material standards ([[as-4100-2020-steel-structures]],
[[as-3600-2018-concrete-structures]]).

`[code]` Referenced documents (Cl 1.2): AS 1170 Parts 1, 2, 4; AS 1250;
AS 1657.

`[derived]` **Currency flag.** The raw copy is the 1996 edition, printed for a
third party with the footer "Document currency not guaranteed when printed"
(3 Jan 2019). Its references to AS 1170.x (SAA Loading Code) and AS 1250 are
to documents since replaced (AS/NZS 1170 series, AS 4100); whether a later
edition of AS 3774 exists was not verified in this ingest and should be
checked on the Standards Australia catalogue before relying on the content
for a live project.
