# Fabrication, erection and modification requirements

> Scope: AS 4100:2020 Section 14 (fabrication: rejection basis, material,
> identification, hole sizes and oversize/slotted hole limits, bolting,
> geometrical tolerances), Section 15 (erection: rejection basis, safety,
> bolted-connection assembly and tensioning — Table 15.2.2.2 minimum bolt
> tension, geometrical tolerances), Section 16 (modification of existing
> structures). All three sections lean heavily on **AS/NZS 5131** for the
> operative detail; AS 4100 sets the outer performance requirements.

## Summary

`[code]` A fabricated or erected item is liable to **rejection** if it
fails material/procedure/tolerance requirements — but may still be
**accepted** if structural adequacy and intended use are demonstrably
unimpaired, or if it passes Section 17 testing (Cl 14.1, 15.1.1).

`[code]` Bolted connections are either **snug tight** or **fully
tensioned** to the Table 15.2.2.2 minimum bolt tension (Cl 15.2.2). Hole
diameter is nominally `d_f + 2 mm` (bolts ≤ 24 mm) or `d_f + 3 mm` (larger),
with defined oversize/slotted-hole limits and washer requirements
(Cl 14.3.2).

## Detail

### Fabrication — general and material (Cl 14.1, 14.2)

`[code]` Rejection grounds (14.1): (a) material fails Cl 14.2; (b)
fabrication fails Cl 14.3; (c) tolerances fail Cl 14.4. Acceptance despite
failure: (i) structural adequacy/intended use demonstrably unimpaired, or
(ii) passes Section 17 testing.

`[code]` Material (14.2): conforms to the Cl 2.2–2.4 product Standards;
surface defects removed per those Standards (14.2.1). Steel grade must be
identifiable at every fabrication stage, or treated as **unidentified
steel** per Cl 2.2.3; marking must not damage the material (14.2.2).

### Fabrication procedures (Cl 14.3)

`[code]` General (14.3.1): fabricate to **AS/NZS 5131**; methods must not
reduce material properties below design values.

`[code]` **Hole size** (14.3.2): completed hole diameter (other than a base
plate hole) = nominal bolt diameter **+ 2 mm** (bolt ≤ 24 mm) or **+ 3 mm**
(bolt > 24 mm). Base-plate holes: ≤ **anchor bolt diameter + 6 mm**; if the
hole is ≥ 3 mm larger than the bolt, use a ≥ 4 mm-thick plate washer under
the nut, fully covering the hole with edge-to-hole-edge distance ≥ 0.5 ×
hole diameter.

`[code]` **Oversize/slotted hole sizes and use**:

| Hole type | Max. size | Use limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Oversize | greater of `1.25 d_f` and `(d_f + 8) mm` diameter | Any/all plies of bearing- or friction-type connections; hardened/plate washer over the hole under both head and nut, edge distance ≥ 0.5 × hole diameter |
| Short slotted | width per hole-size rule above; length = greater of `1.33 d_f` and `(d_f + 10) mm` | Any/all plies, friction- or bearing-type; washer as above. Friction-type: any loading direction. Bearing-type: only where not eccentrically loaded, bolt bears uniformly, slot normal to the design action |
| Long slotted | width per hole-size rule; length ≤ `2.5 d_f` (hole-edge to hole-edge, longest dimension) | Only in **alternate plies**; plate washer ≥ 8 mm thick fully covering the hole under both head and nut. Friction-type: any direction. Bearing-type: only where not eccentric, bolt bears uniformly, slot normal to the load |

`d_f` = nominal bolt diameter. Plate washer material: AS/NZS 3678. (The
plate washer is only needed adjacent to the head/nut that would otherwise
bear directly on the oversized-hole ply.)

`[code]` **Bolting** (14.3.3): bolts/nuts/washers conform to Cl 2.3.1; all
material within the bolt grip must be **steel** (no compressible material
in the grip). Bolt length: ≥ 1 clear thread above the nut, ≥ 1 thread +
run-out clear beneath the nut, after tightening. One washer under the
rotated part; a **tapered washer** where contact-surface slope > 1:20
normal to the bolt axis, with the non-rotating part against the tapered
washer. Nuts in vibration-subject connections must be secured against
loosening (Cl 9.1.6). **Friction-type**: contact surfaces prepared per
AS/NZS 5131; non-conforming surfaces → Cl 9.2.3.2. **Bearing-type**: an
applied finish on contact surfaces is permitted. **Tensioning**: a fully
tensioned high-strength bolt is installed per Cl 15.2.

### Geometrical tolerances — fabrication (Cl 14.4)

`[code]` Per AS/NZS 5131, checked after fabrication **and** any corrosion
protection is applied. Two deviation types: **essential tolerances**
(criteria essential to mechanical resistance/stability, generally
consistent with this Standard's and AS/NZS 5100.6's design-capacity
assumptions) and **functional tolerances** (fit-up/appearance; Class 1 or
Class 2, strictness increasing to Class 2; Class 1 applies by default).
Tolerance measurements exclude coating thickness.

`[code]` **Nonconformance** (14.4.2): essential-tolerance deviations
exceeding the permissible value must be corrected into tolerance, **or**
the excess deviation may be accepted if explicitly included in a revised
design-capacity calculation. Non-essential (functional) tolerance
deviations exceeding the permissible value must simply be corrected.

### Erection — general and procedures (Cl 15.1, 15.2.1)

`[code]` Rejection (15.1.1): (a) fails Cl 15.2 erection requirements; (b)
fails Cl 15.3 tolerances — same accept-nonetheless-if-demonstrated/tested
override as fabrication. Bolts/nuts/washers in the erected structure are
also liable to rejection if they fail Cl 14.3.3 and 15.2, absent a
structural-adequacy demonstration.

`[code]` Safety during erection (15.1.2): steelwork made safe against
erection loading, including erection-equipment/operation loads and wind.

`[code]` Erection procedures (15.2.1): erection and any site modification
to steelwork, per AS/NZS 5131.

### Bolted connection assembly (Cl 15.2.2)

`[code]` Bolted connections are **snug tight** or **fully tensioned**
(15.2.2.1). Tensioning method per Cl 15.2.2.3 (part-turn) or 15.2.2.4
(direct-tension-indication device); the completed connection must reach at
least the Table 15.2.2.2 minimum bolt tension for every bolt in the group
when all are tightened.

`[code]` Table 15.2.2.2 — minimum bolt tension:

| Nominal diameter | 8.8 (kN) | 10.9 (kN) |
|---|---|---|
| M16 | 95 | 130 |
| M20 | 145 | 205 |
| M24 | 210 | 295 |
| M30 | 335 | 465 |
| M36 | 490 | 680 |

(Approximately the AS 4291.1 minimum proof load at 600 MPa (Grade 8.8) or
830 MPa (Grade 10.9) proof stress.)

### Geometrical tolerances — erection (Cl 15.3)

`[code]` Same essential/functional (Class 1/2) framework as Cl 14.4, per
AS/NZS 5131, checked after erection is complete; same nonconformance
options (correct into tolerance, or justify by revised design capacity for
essential tolerances).
