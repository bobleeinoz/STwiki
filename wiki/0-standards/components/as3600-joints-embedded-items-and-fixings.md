# Joints, embedded items and fixings

> Scope: AS 3600 Section 19 in full — construction and movement joints (Cl
> 19.1), embedded items (Cl 19.2), and fixings including post-installed/
> cast-in fastenings (Cl 19.3). No figures or tables in this section.

## Summary

`[code]` A joint must be designed and constructed so the load-carrying
capacity and serviceability of the structure/member is maintained while it
serves its function — either a **construction joint** (for construction
purposes) or a **movement joint** (to control movement) (Cl 19.1.1).

## Detail

### Construction joints (Cl 19.1.2)

`[code]` Designed/installed to suit construction practice, producing a
well-bonded interface between hardened and freshly-placed concrete. Spacing
follows Cl 17.1.3 placement needs and finishing requirements, located in
low-shear regions and unobtrusive locations where possible, driven by
placement/finishing rate or unplanned interruptions. Where an interruption
breaches Cl 17.1.3(c)/(d)/(e), a construction joint is made.

### Movement joints (Cl 19.1.3)

`[code]` Designed/constructed to control movement, control cracking at
design locations, or provide articulation/separation between members —
without impairing load-carrying capacity or serviceability. They accommodate
shrinkage, creep, diurnal and differential-settlement movement; generally
extend through the full member depth (except shrinkage joints, which need
not). Spacing accounts for shrinkage, temperature, moisture, creep and (in
prestressed members) member shortening from prestress.

### Joint fillers and sealants (Cl 19.1.4)

`[code]` Infill material must stay in place and deform with loading/movement
without functional degradation — a filler, a sealant, or both. Fillers may
be inserted compressed, either while the concrete is plastic or after
hardening. A sealant provides weatherproofing, fire resistance, acoustic
insulation, chemical resistance, or ingress prevention beyond a filler's
capability. Joints are sealed/detailed to exclude dirt/incompressible
material that would impair movement/operation; where the joint carries load,
infill hardness and concrete-edge protection must prevent edge spalling.

### Embedded items (Cl 19.2)

`[code]` Embedded items include pipes/conduits with fittings, sleeves,
permanent fixing inserts, holding-down bolts and other supports (Cl 19.2.1).
They may be embedded provided member strength/serviceability is satisfied
and Cl 4.10.3.7 durability is met. Embedded conduits/pipes conform with the
relevant Standard (e.g. AS/NZS 3000 electrical, AS/NZS 3500 plumbing) (Cl
19.2.2); pressure/temperature-service pipes are permitted if maximum
pressure ≤2000 kPa and the pipe's effect on member strength/serviceability is
accounted for. Minimum clear spacing between embedded items, and between
embedded items and bars/tendons/ducts, must allow concrete placement and
compaction per Cl 17.1.3 (Cl 19.2.3).

### Fixings (Cl 19.3)

`[code]` Fixings — holding-down bolts, inserts, ferrules — are designed to
transmit all forces likely to act on them (Cl 19.3.1(a)); lifting inserts/
temporary-support fixings comply with AS 3850.1; fixings are designed to
yield before ultimate failure under overload. Anchorage design per Section
13, with design strength = `φ × ultimate strength` (`φ = 0.6`); shallow
anchorages need a concrete cone-failure check accounting for edge distance,
spacing, reinforcement effect, and concrete strength at time of loading. In
the absence of calculation, strength is set by Appendix B Paragraph B4
prototype load testing to failure — design strength = `φ × ultimate strength`
(ultimate = average failure load ÷ the Table B4.3 factor, `φ = 0.6`).

![[as3600-table-b4.3-variability-factor-production-units.png]]
*Table B4.3 — factor to allow for variability in production of units, by
number of similar units tested and expected coefficient of variation
(AS 3600:2018).*

Fixing spacing/cover follows Cl 19.2.3 and Section 4 (fire cover: Section
5).

`[code]` Post-installed and cast-in fastenings using mechanical, chemical or
cast-in anchor channels are designed to AS 5216 (Cl 19.3.2). Cast-in fixings
are designed to AS 3850.1, Appendix B (Cl 19.3.3).
