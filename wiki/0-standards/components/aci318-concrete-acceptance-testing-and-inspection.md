# ACI 318M-19 acceptance of hardened concrete, strength-test investigation and inspection

> Scope: ACI 318M-19 Cl 26.12 (evaluation and acceptance) and Cl 26.13 (inspection). **ACI 318M-19
> page, kept separate from the AS 3600:2018 pages** (see [[aci-318m-19-building-code-concrete]]).
> Preceding construction rules: [[aci318-construction-documents-concrete-materials-and-production]],
> [[aci318-reinforcement-anchors-embedments-precast-and-formwork-requirements]]. Existing-structure strength
> evaluation, the next step if cores fail, is in [[aci318-strength-evaluation-of-existing-structures]].

## Summary

`[code]` Acceptance of concrete is based on standard-cured strength tests with frequency and statistical
criteria; failures trigger core testing and, if still in doubt, a Chapter 27 evaluation. Inspection is
as required by the general building code, with Chapter 26 minimums.

## Detail

### Strength tests (Cl 26.12.1)

`[code]` A strength test is the average of ≥ 2 cylinders of 150×300 mm or ≥ 3 of 100×200 mm from one
sample (ASTM C172 at the point of delivery), standard-cured (ASTM C31), tested per ASTM C39 at 28 days or
the designated age (Cl 26.12.1.1(a)). For shotcrete: average of ≥ 3 cores of 75 mm nominal diameter from a
test panel (ASTM C1140) at 28 days or the designated age (Cl 26.12.1.1(b)). Testing agency complies with ASTM
C1077; certified field and laboratory technicians; reports go to the design professional, contractor, producer
and, on request, owner and building official (Cl 26.12.1.1(c)–(f)).

### Frequency (Cl 26.12.2)

`[code]` Sample each mixture placed each day at least: once a day; once per **110 m³**; and once per **460 m²**
of slab or wall surface (Cl 26.12.2.1(a)). If that gives fewer than five tests for a mixture, take specimens
from at least five random batches or every batch if fewer than five (Cl 26.12.2.1(b)). Mixtures totalling less than
**38 m³** need no strength tests if satisfactory strength evidence is approved by the building official
(Cl 26.12.2.1(c)). Shotcrete: a test panel per mixture and nozzle operator at least once per day or every
38 m³, whichever gives more panels (Cl 26.12.2.1(d)).

### Acceptance criteria (Cl 26.12.3–26.12.5)

`[code]` **Standard-cured specimens** (Cl 26.12.3.1): the mixture is acceptable if (1) every average of any
three consecutive strength tests ≥ `f'c`, and (2) no single test falls below `f'c` by more than 3.5 MPa
when `f'c ≤ 35 MPa`, or by more than `0.10f'c` when `f'c > 35 MPa`. If either fails, take steps to raise
subsequent strengths; failure of (2) invokes the Cl 26.12.6 investigation.

`[code]` **Shotcrete** (Cl 26.12.4.1): panels shot in the same orientation by the same operator; cores per ASTM
C1604; acceptable if every average of three consecutive panels ≥ `f'c`, and the average of three cores from one
panel ≥ `0.85f'c` with no core < `0.75f'c`.

`[code]` **Lightweight concrete density** (Cl 26.12.5.1): sampled at the point of delivery (ASTM C172) and the
frequency of Cl 26.12.2, measured by ASTM C138; acceptable within **±65 kg/m³** of the fresh density
corresponding to the specified equilibrium density unless otherwise permitted.

### Investigation of low strength (Cl 26.12.6)

`[code]` If a standard-cured test fails by more than the allowed margin, or field-cured cylinders show
inadequate protection/curing, steps are taken to ensure structural adequacy (Cl 26.12.6.1(a)). If low-strength
concrete is confirmed and calculations show significantly reduced adequacy, **three cores** are drilled per
failing test per ASTM C42 (details modifiable by the design professional or building official); cores are
moisture-conditioned in watertight bags, and tested between 5 days after last wetting and 7 days after coring
unless otherwise approved (Cl 26.12.6.1(b)–(d)). The area is adequate if the three-core average ≥ `0.85f'c` and no
core < `0.75f'c` (Cl 26.12.6.1(e)); extra cores may be taken for erratic results (Cl 26.12.6.1(f)). If still in doubt,
the responsible authority may order a Chapter 27 strength evaluation or other action (Cl 26.12.6.1(g)).

### Steel fibre-reinforced concrete for shear (Cl 26.12.7)

`[code]` Must meet the standard-cured compressive-strength criteria and ASTM C1609 residual strengths: at
midspan deflection of **span/300** at least the greater of 90 % of the first-peak strength and 90 % of the
strength corresponding to `0.62√f'c`; at **span/150** at least the greater of 75 % of the first-peak strength and
75 % of the strength corresponding to `0.62√f'c` (Cl 26.12.7.1(a)).

### Inspection (Cl 26.13)

`[code]` Concrete construction is inspected as the general building code requires, with Chapter 26 as the minimum;
absent a general code, per this Code (Cl 26.13.1.1). Inspection is by the responsible licensed design professional, a
person under their supervision, or a qualified inspector, verifying conformance to construction documents
throughout; an inspector inspecting formwork, placement, reinforcement and embedments must be certified
(Cl 26.13.1.2). Placement and reinforcement for special moment frames, special-wall boundary elements, coupling
beams, and precast diaphragms with moderate/high-deformability connections in SDC C–F are inspected under the
supervision of the design professional, by a person with demonstrated capability, or by a certified inspector;
precast-diaphragm connection tolerances per ACI 550.5M (Cl 26.13.1.3). Welding inspection by a qualified welding
inspector per AWS D1.4, with weldability of non-A706 bars confirmed (Cl 26.13.1.4). Post-installed expansion, screw
and undercut anchors: certified inspector (or one specifically approved) (Cl 26.13.1.5); all adhesive anchors:
certified inspector (Cl 26.13.1.6).

`[code]` **Reports (Cl 26.13.2).** Reports document inspected items through each stage and are kept ≥ **2 years**
after completion (Cl 26.13.2.1). Contents: progress; significant construction loads on completed elements; mixing
date/time, quantity, mixture identification, approximate location and fresh/hardened test results; concrete
temperatures and protection if ambient is below 4 °C or above 35 °C; reinforcement placement and tendon
tensioning with measured elongation and gauge force (Cl 26.13.2.2). A615 mill test reports are verified for special
seismic systems; weldability test reports for non-A706 bars; post-installed and adhesive anchor materials and
installation verified against approved documents and MPII, with proof-load procedures and results where required
(Cl 26.13.2.3–26.13.2.5).

**Items for inspection (Cl 26.13.3).** `[code]` Unless the general building code says otherwise:
- *Continuous inspection* (Cl 26.13.3.2): concrete mixture for the intended location before placing; tensioning and
  grouting of tendons; reinforcement placement in special moment frames, special-wall boundary elements and
  coupling beams; their welding; post-installed anchors where required by the anchor assessment or for adhesive
  anchors in horizontal/upward orientation under sustained tension.
- *Periodic inspection* (Cl 26.13.3.3): placement of reinforcement, embedments and tendons; other welding; curing
  method and duration; construction and removal of forms and reshoring; precast erection sequence and
  connections; in-place strength before stressing tendons or removing shores/forms from beams and slabs; cast-in
  anchors and anchor reinforcement tolerances; installation of expansion/screw/undercut anchors and other adhesive
  anchors; proof loading of anchors.
