# Prefabricated concrete structures

> Scope: AS 3600 Cl 17.7 — minimum structural-integrity provisions for
> prefabricated (precast) concrete structures: floor/roof diaphragm
> connections, vertical integrity ties, loadbearing wall systems, grouted
> joint ducts, and floor-element seating.

## Summary

`[code]` Except where Cl 17.7.4 (prefabricated loadbearing walls) governs,
the minimum structural-integrity provisions of Cl 17.7.1–17.7.3 apply to all
prefabricated concrete structures.

## Detail

### Diaphragm connections and vertical ties (Cl 17.7.2–17.7.3)

`[code]` Connections between prefabricated diaphragm elements and the
members they laterally support need a designed tensile strength ≥4.5 kN per
linear metre (Cl 17.7.2).

`[code]` Vertical integrity ties (Cl 17.7.3), for all vertical structural
members except non-loadbearing elements, at horizontal joints:
(a) prefabricated-column connections — designed tensile strength ≥`fvt Ag`
(`fvt = 1.4 MPa`, `Ag` in mm²; oversized columns may use a reduced effective
area, not less than half the actual total area);
(b) prefabricated wall panels — ≥2 ties per panel, each ≥45 kN;
(c) where design forces give no base tension, the ties in (b) anchor into a
reinforced slab-on-ground or footing.
Connections relying solely on gravity friction are barred.

### Loadbearing walls, grouted ducts, seating (Cl 17.7.4–17.7.6)

`[code]` Prefabricated loadbearing wall structures (Cl 17.7.4) need ties
between structural elements meeting the loadbearing wall system's
requirements, designed by mechanics principles.

`[code]` Grouted ducts joining prefabricated elements (Cl 17.7.5) must be
fully confined by Ductility Class N reinforcement anchored perpendicular to
the duct per Section 11 (see
[[as3600-wall-reinforcement-requirements]]).

`[code]` Seating for prefabricated floor elements (Cl 17.7.6) must be at
least 1.5× the Ultimate Limit State drift.
