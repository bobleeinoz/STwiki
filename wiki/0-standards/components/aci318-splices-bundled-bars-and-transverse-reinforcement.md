# ACI 318M-19 splices, bundled bars and transverse reinforcement

> Scope: ACI 318M-19 Cl 25.5–25.7. Spacing, hooks and development lengths are in
> [[aci318-reinforcement-spacing-hooks-and-development]]. **ACI 318M-19 page, kept
> separate from the AS 3600:2018 pages** (see [[aci-318m-19-building-code-concrete]]);
> AS 3600 comparison: [[as3600-development-length-of-reinforcement]].

## Summary

`[code]` Lap, end-bearing, mechanical and welded splices (Cl 25.5), bundled
nonprestressed bars and post-tensioning ducts (Cl 25.6), and the anchorage and
arrangement rules for stirrups, ties, spirals and hoops (Cl 25.7).

## Detail

### General splice rules (Cl 25.5.1)

`[code]` No lap splices of bars larger than No. 36, except No. 43/57 to No. 36 or smaller in
compression (Cl 25.5.1.1, 25.5.5.3). Contact splices keep the Cl 25.2.1 clear spacing to
adjacent bars/splices (Cl 25.5.1.2). Noncontact flexural splices: transverse spacing of spliced
bars ≤ lesser of one-fifth the lap length and 150 mm (Cl 25.5.1.3). The excess-reinforcement
reduction of Cl 25.4.10.1 is not allowed when computing lap lengths (Cl 25.5.1.4). For
`f_y ≥ 550 MPa` bars closer than 150 mm centres, `K_tr ≥ 0.5d_b` (Cl 25.5.1.5). Shotcrete:
noncontact splices of No. 19 and smaller need clear spacing ≥ greater of `6d_b` and 65 mm; No. 22
and larger and contact splices are justified by mock-up panels, with contact splices oriented
perpendicular to the surface (Cl 25.5.1.6–25.5.1.7). Bundled-bar laps follow Cl 25.6.1.7
(Cl 25.5.1.8).

### Tension lap splices — deformed bars and wires (Cl 25.5.2)

![[aci318-table-25.5.2.1-tension-lap-splice-lengths.png]]
*Table 25.5.2.1 — `A_s,provided/A_s,required ≥ 2.0`: Class A (≤ 50 % spliced within the lap
length) `ℓ_st` = greater of `1.0ℓ_d` and 300 mm, Class B (100 %) greater of `1.3ℓ_d` and 300 mm;
`< 2.0`: Class B in all cases (ACI 318M-19).* `ℓ_d` is per Cl 25.4.2.1(a). Different-sized bars:
`ℓ_st` = greater of `ℓ_d` of the larger bar and the splice length of the smaller bar
(Cl 25.5.2.2).

![[aci318-figure-R25.5.2.1-staggered-splice-spacing.png]]
*Fig. R25.5.2.1 — clear spacing taken between adjacent staggered splices when computing `ℓ_d`
(ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

### Welded wire reinforcement splices (Cl 25.5.3–25.5.4)

`[code]` **Welded deformed wire** with cross wires in the lap: `ℓ_st` ≥ greater of `1.3ℓ_d` and
200 mm (`ℓ_d` per Cl 25.4.6.1(a)), provided outermost cross wires of each sheet overlap ≥ 50 mm
and all wires are MD200 or smaller; otherwise Cl 25.5.2 or 25.5.4 governs; galvanized sheets
follow Cl 25.5.4 (Cl 25.5.3.1). **Welded plain wire:** between outermost cross wires `ℓ_st` ≥
greatest of `s + 50 mm`, `1.5ℓ_d` (per Cl 25.4.7.2(b)) and 150 mm; where
`A_s,provided/A_s,required ≥ 2.0`, the greater of `1.5ℓ_d` and 50 mm (Cl 25.5.4.1–25.5.4.2).

### Compression lap and end-bearing splices (Cl 25.5.5–25.5.6)

`[code]` Compression lap `ℓ_sc` for No. 36 and smaller (Cl 25.5.5.1): `f_y ≤ 420 MPa` → longer of
`0.071f_yd_b` and 300 mm; `420 < f_y ≤ 550` → longer of `(0.13f_y − 24)d_b` and 300 mm;
`f_y > 550` → longer of `(0.13f_y − 24)d_b` and `ℓ_st` from Table 25.5.2.1; increase by one-third
where `f'c < 21 MPa`. Different sizes: longer of `ℓ_dc` of the larger bar and `ℓ_sc` of the
smaller (Cl 25.5.5.4). End-bearing splices are allowed for bars in compression only, in members
with closed stirrups/ties/spirals/hoops, with square-cut ends within 1.5° of perpendicular and
fitted within 3° of full bearing (Cl 25.5.6.1–25.5.6.3).

### Mechanical and welded splices (Cl 25.5.7)

`[code]` Must develop ≥ **1.25 `f_y`** in tension or compression (Cl 25.5.7.1); welding per
Cl 26.6.4 (Cl 25.5.7.2). Staggering is not required except for tension ties, where mechanical or
welded splices are mandatory and adjacent-bar splices are staggered ≥ 750 mm
(Cl 25.5.7.3–25.5.7.4).

### Bundled reinforcement (Cl 25.6)

`[code]` Bundles of up to **four** parallel bars in contact, enclosed by transverse reinforcement
(at least No. 13 in compression members); bars larger than No. 36 are not bundled in beams
(Cl 25.6.1.1–25.6.1.3). Bars cut off within a flexural span stagger ≥ `40d_b` (Cl 25.6.1.4).
Development length of each bar in a bundle is increased 20 % for three bars and 33 % for four
(Cl 25.6.1.5). A bundle is treated as one equivalent-area bar for spacing, cover,
Table 25.4.2.3 conditions, the confinement term and ψ_e (Cl 25.6.1.6). Bundle laps use the
increased individual lap length, individual splices do not overlap, and whole bundles are not
lapped (Cl 25.6.1.7). Post-tensioning ducts may be bundled if concrete can be placed and
tendons are prevented from breaking out of the duct (Cl 25.6.2.1).

### Stirrups (Cl 25.7.1)

`[code]` Stirrups extend as close to the compression and tension faces as cover and other
steel allow and are anchored at both ends; as shear reinforcement they extend a distance `d`
from the extreme compression fibre (Cl 25.7.1.1). Each bend of a continuous stirrup encloses a
longitudinal bar or strand (Cl 25.7.1.2). Anchorage (Cl 25.7.1.3): (a) No. 16/MD200 and smaller,
and Nos. 19–25 with `f_yt ≤ 280 MPa` — standard hook around a longitudinal bar; (b) Nos. 19–25
with `f_yt > 280 MPa` — hook plus embedment from mid-height to the hook end ≥
`0.17d_bf_yt/(λ√f'c)`; (c) joists, No. 13/MD130 and smaller — standard hook. Welded wire U-stirrup
and single-leg anchorage rules are in Cl 25.7.1.4–25.7.1.5.

Torsion or integrity stirrups are closed, perpendicular to the member axis, and anchored by
135° standard hooks around a longitudinal bar, or by Cl 25.7.1.3/25.7.1.4 where a flange or slab
restrains spalling (Cl 25.7.1.6); they may be a U-stirrup plus a crosstie whose 90° hook is
restrained (Cl 25.7.1.6.1).

![[aci318-figure-R25.7.1.6-torsion-corner-spalling.png]]
*Fig. R25.7.1.6 — corner spalling in torsion and restraint by a slab (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

Other closed stirrups may be made from two U-stirrups lapped ≥ `1.3ℓ_d`; in members at least
450 mm deep, laps with `A_bf_yt ≤ 40 kN` per leg are adequate if the legs run the full available
depth (Cl 25.7.1.7).

![[aci318-figure-R25.7.1.7-closed-stirrup-configurations.png]]
*Fig. R25.7.1.7 — closed stirrup configurations from paired U-stirrups (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

### Ties (Cl 25.7.2)

`[code]` Closed loops of deformed bar with clear spacing ≥ `(4/3)d_agg` and centre-to-centre
spacing ≤ least of `16d_b` (longitudinal), `48d_b` (tie) and the smallest member dimension
(Cl 25.7.2.1). Minimum tie size: No. 10 around No. 32 and smaller longitudinal bars, No. 13
around No. 36 and larger or bundled bars (Cl 25.7.2.2). Rectilinear ties: every corner and
alternate longitudinal bar is supported by a tie corner with included angle ≤ 135°, and no
unsupported bar is more than 150 mm clear from a supported bar along the tie (Cl 25.7.2.3).
Anchorage is by standard hooks (Cl 25.3.2) engaging a longitudinal bar; ties are not made from
interlocking headed bars (Cl 25.7.2.3.1).

![[aci318-figure-R25.7.2.3a-tie-support-measurements.png]]
*Fig. R25.7.2.3a — lateral support and 150 mm clear-spacing measurements, with crosstie
arrangements (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

![[aci318-figure-R25.7.2.3b-continuous-tie-anchorage.png]]
*Fig. R25.7.2.3b — continuously wound tie anchorage (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

`[code]` Circular ties: ends overlap ≥ 150 mm, end with standard hooks engaging a longitudinal
bar, and overlaps of adjacent ties are staggered around the perimeter (Cl 25.7.2.4–25.7.2.4.1).
Torsion ties are perpendicular to the member axis with 135° or seismic hooks, or per
Cl 25.7.1.3/25.7.1.4 where spalling is restrained (Cl 25.7.2.5).

![[aci318-figure-R25.7.2.4.1-circular-tie-anchorage.png]]
*Fig. R25.7.2.4.1 — circular tie anchorage with staggered hook locations (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

### Spirals (Cl 25.7.3)

`[code]` Evenly spaced continuous bar or wire, clear spacing ≥ greater of 25 mm and `(4/3)d_agg`
and ≤ 75 mm (Cl 25.7.3.1); diameter ≥ 9.5 mm in cast-in-place construction (Cl 25.7.3.2).
Volumetric ratio (except deep-foundation transverse steel), with `f_yt ≤ 690 MPa`
(Cl 25.7.3.3):

`ρ_s ≥ 0.45 (A_g/A_ch − 1) f'c/f_yt` (Eq. 25.7.3.3)

Anchorage: 1½ extra turns at each end (Cl 25.7.3.4). Splices: mechanical/welded per Cl 25.5.7,
or laps per Table 25.7.3.6 (not less than 300 mm) for `f_yt ≤ 420 MPa` (Cl 25.7.3.5–25.7.3.6).

![[aci318-table-25.7.3.6-spiral-lap-length.png]]
*Table 25.7.3.6 — spiral lap: `48d_b` for uncoated or galvanized deformed bar/wire without a
hook; `72d_b` for epoxy-coated deformed bars/wire and for plain bar/wire without a hook; `48d_b`
with a standard hook (Cl 25.3.2) embedded in the core (ACI 318M-19).*

![[aci318-figure-R25.7.3.4-spiral-anchorage.png]]
*Fig. R25.7.3.4 — spiral anchorage with 1½ extra turns (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

### Hoops (Cl 25.7.4)

`[code]` A closed tie or continuously wound tie, possibly made of several elements each with
seismic hooks at both ends; hook ends engage a longitudinal bar (Cl 25.3.4) and hoops are not
made from interlocking headed bars (Cl 25.7.4.1–25.7.4.2). Seismic use:
[[aci318-special-moment-frames]], [[aci318-special-structural-walls]].
