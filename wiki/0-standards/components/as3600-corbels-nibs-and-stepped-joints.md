# Additional requirements for corbels, nibs and stepped joints

> Scope: AS 3600 Cl 12.3 (corbels and continuous nibs supporting other
> members) and Cl 12.4 (stepped joints in beams and slabs) — detailing rules
> layered on top of the Cl 12.2 strut-and-tie model or other Section 12
> strength design.

## Summary

`[code]` Both clauses address the same underlying issue from two different
geometries: a supported member restrained horizontally (by movement,
shrinkage, temperature or prestress effects) imposes a horizontal force on
the supporting detail that must be assessed and reinforced for — in both
cases, that horizontal force is **not to be taken as less than 20% of the
vertical force**, regardless of what a more detailed assessment might
suggest.

## Detail

### Corbels and continuous nibs (Cl 12.3)

`[code]` Where corbels/nibs support other members, additional requirements:

- **Anchorage of tensile reinforcement** (a): anchored at the nib/corbel's
  free end by welded/mechanical anchorage, or by a loop (vertical or
  horizontal plane) — where looped, the loaded area must not project beyond
  the straight portion of that reinforcement.
- **Horizontal force** (b): assessed from movement/shrinkage/temperature/
  prestress effects in the supported member, floored at 20% of the vertical
  force.
- **Line of action of the load** (c): at the outside edge of a bearing pad
  for continuous nibs; at one-third the bearing width from the free end for
  a corbel. Without a bearing pad: at the start of any edge chamfer, or the
  outside face of the nib/corbel.
- **Spalling protection** (d): where a flexural member is supported, the
  nib's outside face must be protected against spalling.

### Stepped joints in beams and slabs (Cl 12.4)

`[code]` Additional requirements:

- **Horizontal force** (a): same 20%-of-vertical-force floor as corbels/nibs,
  from the same causes (movement, shrinkage, temperature, prestress, other).
- **Prestressed members** (b): the vertical component of the prestressing
  force itself is ignored in this assessment (own-words: the step's local
  design doesn't get to claim prestress's vertical component as a helpful
  effect here, even where it genuinely would reduce shear demand elsewhere).
- **Horizontal reinforcement extent** (c): extends at least the beam depth
  `D` beyond the step, anchored beyond the plane of any potential shear
  crack.
- **Hanging reinforcement** (d): placed as close as possible to the step's
  vertical face.
