# ACI 318M-19 reinforcement spacing, standard hooks and development lengths

> Scope: ACI 318M-19 Cl 25.1–25.4 — minimum bar and tendon spacing, standard
> and seismic hooks, crossties, and development of bars, wires, hooks, heads and
> strand. Splices, bundles and transverse reinforcement are in
> [[aci318-splices-bundled-bars-and-transverse-reinforcement]]; post-tensioning
> anchorages in [[aci318-post-tensioning-anchorages-and-anchorage-zones]].
> **ACI 318M-19 page, kept separate from the AS 3600:2018 pages**
> (see [[aci-318m-19-building-code-concrete]]). AS 3600 counterpart:
> [[as3600-development-length-of-reinforcement]]. ACI bar sizes are metric designations
> (No. 10–No. 57), not AS/NZS 4671 sizes.

## Summary

`[code]` Chapter 25 covers minimum spacing, standard hooks, seismic hooks and
crossties, development of reinforcement, splices, bundled reinforcement,
transverse reinforcement, and post-tensioning anchorages and couplers
(Cl 25.1.1); anchorage zones for post-tensioned tendons are in Cl 25.9
(Cl 25.1.2). Development lengths do not take a strength reduction factor φ
(Cl 25.4.1.3).

## Detail

### Minimum spacing (Cl 25.2)

`[code]` Nonprestressed bars in a horizontal layer: clear spacing ≥ greatest of
25 mm, `d_b`, `(4/3)d_agg` (Cl 25.2.1). Multiple layers: upper bars directly above
lower bars, clear spacing between layers ≥ 25 mm (Cl 25.2.2). Longitudinal bars in
columns, pedestals, struts and wall boundary elements: clear spacing ≥ greatest of
40 mm, `1.5d_b`, `(4/3)d_agg` (Cl 25.2.3). Pretensioned strand at member ends:
centre-to-centre spacing ≥ greater of Table 25.2.4 and `(4/3)d_agg + d_b`
(Cl 25.2.4); pretensioned wire ≥ greater of `5d_b` and `(4/3)d_agg + d_b`
(Cl 25.2.5). Reduced vertical spacing and bundling of prestressed steel is allowed
in the middle part of a span (Cl 25.2.6).

![[aci318-table-25.2.4-pretensioned-strand-spacing.png]]
*Table 25.2.4 — pretensioned strand spacing at member ends: `f'ci < 28 MPa` → `4d_b` for
all sizes; `f'ci ≥ 28 MPa` → `4d_b` below 12.7 mm, 45 mm for 12.7 mm, 50 mm for 15.2 mm
(ACI 318M-19).*

`[code]` **Shotcrete** (Cl 25.2.7–25.2.10): clear bar spacing ≥ greater of `6d_b` and
65 mm; with two curtains, the curtain nearer the nozzle ≥ `12d_b`; other spacings need
mock-up panels proving encasement, with the licensed design professional specifying the
panel quantity, frequency per nozzleman/member type and thickness. Prestressing strand and
wire in shotcrete follow Cl 25.2.4/25.2.5; ties, hoops and spirals in shotcrete columns
need ≥ 75 mm clear spacing unless mock-ups demonstrate encasement.

### Hooks, seismic hooks and crossties (Cl 25.3)

![[aci318-table-25.3.1-standard-hook-geometry-tension.png]]
*Table 25.3.1 — standard hooks for deformed bars in tension: 90° hook extension `12d_b`;
180° hook extension greater of `4d_b` and 65 mm; minimum inside bend diameter `6d_b`
(Nos. 10–25), `8d_b` (Nos. 29–36), `10d_b` (Nos. 43–57) (ACI 318M-19).* A longer
extension is permitted but is not credited as extra anchorage.

![[aci318-table-25.3.2-stirrup-tie-hoop-hooks.png]]
*Table 25.3.2 — stirrups, ties and hoops: bend diameter `4d_b` (Nos. 10–16) or `6d_b`
(Nos. 19–25); extension greater of `6d_b` and 75 mm for 90° (Nos. 10–16) and 135° hooks,
`12d_b` for 90° Nos. 19–25, greater of `4d_b` and 65 mm for 180° (ACI 318M-19).* Standard
hooks enclose longitudinal reinforcement (Cl 25.3.2).

`[code]` Welded wire reinforcement stirrups/ties: bend diameter ≥ `4d_b` for deformed
wire larger than MD35 and `2d_b` otherwise; bends under `8d_b` diameter ≥ `4d_b` from
the nearest welded intersection (Cl 25.3.3). Seismic hooks: ≥ 90° for circular hoops and
135° for other hoops, engaging longitudinal bars with the extension projecting into the
stirrup/hoop interior (Cl 25.3.4). Crossties: continuous, a seismic hook at one end and a
standard hook (≥ 90°) at the other, hooks engaging peripheral longitudinal bars, and 90°
hooks of successive crossties engaging the same bar alternated end for end unless
Cl 18.6.4.3 or 25.7.1.6.1 applies (Cl 25.3.5).

![[aci318-figure-R25.3.5-crosstie.png]]
*Fig. R25.3.5 — crosstie with alternate hook positions (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

### Development — general (Cl 25.4.1)

`[code]` Calculated bar force at each section is developed on both sides by embedment,
hook, head, mechanical device or a combination (Cl 25.4.1.1). Hooks and heads do not develop
bars in compression (Cl 25.4.1.2). `√f'c` used for development is capped at **8.3 MPa**
(Cl 25.4.1.4).

### Deformed bars and wires in tension (Cl 25.4.2)

`[code]` `ℓ_d` is the greater of the calculated length (Cl 25.4.2.3 or 25.4.2.4 with
Table 25.4.2.5 factors) and **300 mm** (Cl 25.4.2.1). For `f_y ≥ 550 MPa` bars spaced
closer than 150 mm centre to centre, provide transverse steel with `K_tr ≥ 0.5d_b`
(Cl 25.4.2.2).

![[aci318-table-25.4.2.3-development-length-tension.png]]
*Table 25.4.2.3 — simplified `ℓ_d` (× `d_b`): Nos. 19 and smaller, spacing/cover/stirrup
conditions met: `f_yψ_tψ_eψ_g/(2.1λ√f'c)`; Nos. 22 and larger: `f_yψ_tψ_eψ_g/(1.7λ√f'c)`.
Other cases: `/(1.4λ√f'c)` and `/(1.1λ√f'c)` respectively (ACI 318M-19).*

![[aci318-eq-25.4.2.4-development-length-general.png]]
*Eq. 25.4.2.4a/b — general `ℓ_d` with confinement term `(c_b + K_tr)/d_b ≤ 2.5` and
`K_tr = 40A_tr/(sn)`; `K_tr = 0` may always be used as a simplification (ACI 318M-19).*

![[aci318-table-25.4.2.5-modification-factors-tension.png]]
*Table 25.4.2.5 — modification factors: λ (0.75 lightweight / 1.0); ψ_g (1.0 Gr 280/420,
1.15 Gr 550, 1.3 Gr 690); ψ_e (1.5 epoxy with cover < `3d_b` or spacing < `6d_b`, 1.2
other epoxy, 1.0 uncoated/galvanized); ψ_s (0.8 for No. 19 and smaller, 1.0 larger);
ψ_t (1.3 with more than 300 mm fresh concrete below, else 1.0); the product ψ_tψ_e need
not exceed 1.7 (ACI 318M-19).*

### Standard hooks in tension (Cl 25.4.3)

`[code]` `ℓ_dh` is the greatest of Eq. 25.4.3.1a, `8d_b` and 150 mm (Cl 25.4.3.1):

![[aci318-eq-25.4.3.1-hooked-bar-development-length.png]]
*Eq. 25.4.3.1a — `ℓ_dh = (f_yψ_eψ_rψ_oψ_c/(23λ√f'c)) d_b^1.5` (ACI 318M-19).*

![[aci318-table-25.4.3.2-modification-factors-hooked-bars.png]]
*Table 25.4.3.2 — hooked bar factors: λ; ψ_e (1.2 epoxy); ψ_r (1.0 with `A_th ≥ 0.4A_hs` or
`s ≥ 6d_b`, otherwise 1.6, No. 36 and smaller); ψ_o (1.0 terminating in column core with
side cover ≥ 65 mm or side cover ≥ `6d_b`, otherwise 1.25); ψ_c (`f'c/105 + 0.6` for
`f'c < 42 MPa`, else 1.0) (ACI 318M-19).*

`[code]` `A_th` is made up of ties/stirrups enclosing the hook per Cl 25.3.2, or other
reinforcement extending ≥ `0.75ℓ_dh` along the bar and either parallel (two or more,
spacing ≤ `8d_b`, within `15d_b` of the bar axis) or perpendicular (two or more along
`ℓ_dh`, spacing ≤ `8d_b`) (Cl 25.4.3.3).

![[aci318-figure-R25.4.3.3a-hook-confinement-parallel.png]]
*Fig. R25.4.3.3a — confining reinforcement parallel to the hooked bar (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

![[aci318-figure-R25.4.3.3b-hook-confinement-perpendicular.png]]
*Fig. R25.4.3.3b — confining reinforcement perpendicular to the hooked bar (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

`[code]` At discontinuous member ends with side and top/bottom cover both < 65 mm, the
hook is enclosed along `ℓ_dh` by ties/stirrups perpendicular to `ℓ_dh` at `s ≤ 3d_b`, the
first within `2d_b` of the outside of the bend (Cl 25.4.3.4).

![[aci318-figure-R25.4.3.4-cover-discontinuous-end.png]]
*Fig. R25.4.3.4 — cover condition triggering Cl 25.4.3.4 (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

### Headed deformed bars in tension (Cl 25.4.4)

`[code]` A head may develop a bar if: bar conforms to Cl 20.2.1.6; size ≤ No. 36; net bearing
area `A_brg ≥ 4A_b`; normalweight concrete; clear cover ≥ `2d_b`; centre-to-centre spacing ≥
`3d_b` (Cl 25.4.4.1). `ℓ_dt` is the longest of Eq. 25.4.4.2a, `8d_b` and 150 mm:

![[aci318-eq-25.4.4.2-headed-bar-development-length.png]]
*Eq. 25.4.4.2a — `ℓ_dt = (f_yψ_eψ_pψ_oψ_c/(31√f'c)) d_b^1.5` (no λ term, as lightweight concrete
is excluded) (ACI 318M-19).*

![[aci318-table-25.4.4.3-modification-factors-headed-bars.png]]
*Table 25.4.4.3 — headed bar factors: ψ_e (1.2 epoxy); ψ_p (1.0 with `A_tt ≥ 0.3A_hs` or
`s ≥ 6d_b`, else 1.6); ψ_o (1.0 in column core with side cover ≥ 65 mm or ≥ `6d_b`, else 1.25);
ψ_c as for hooks (ACI 318M-19).*

![[aci318-figure-R25.4.4.2a-headed-bar-development.png]]
*Fig. R25.4.4.2a — development of headed deformed bars (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

`[code]` In beam–column joints `A_tt` is the parallel tie area within `8d_b` of the bar toward
the joint middle (Cl 25.4.4.4); elsewhere ties are ignored and ψ_p = 1.0 when spacing ≥ `6d_b`
(Cl 25.4.4.5). Where headed bars carry beam negative moment into a joint, the column extends
above the joint by ≥ the joint depth `h`, or equivalent vertical joint reinforcement encloses
the bars (Cl 25.4.4.6).

![[aci318-figure-R25.4.4.4-headed-bar-joint-ties.png]]
*Fig. R25.4.4.4 — parallel ties for headed bars in a beam–column joint (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

### Mechanical anchorage and welded wire (Cl 25.4.5–25.4.7)

`[code]` Any mechanical device that develops `f_y` is acceptable if approved by the building
official (Cl 25.4.5.1). **Welded deformed wire reinforcement:** `ℓ_d` ≥ 200 mm, computed from
Cl 25.4.2.3/25.4.2.4 × `ψ_w`, for wires MD200 or smaller (Cl 25.4.6.1–25.4.6.2);
`ψ_w` = greater of `(f_y − 240)/f_y` and `5d_b/s`, ≤ 1.0, when a cross wire lies within `ℓ_d`
at least 50 mm from the critical section, otherwise 1.0 (Cl 25.4.6.3–25.4.6.4); plain wires,
wires larger than MD200 and galvanized wire follow Cl 25.4.7 (Cl 25.4.6.5–25.4.6.6).
**Welded plain wire reinforcement:** `ℓ_d` ≥ 150 mm with at least two cross wires within `ℓ_d`,
and `ℓ_d` = greater of (cross-wire spacing + 50 mm) and `3.3(f_y/(λ√f'c))(A_b/s)`
(Cl 25.4.7.1–25.4.7.2). (The second formula was read from the rendered page; confirm
against the printed Cl 25.4.7.2(b) before use.)

### Pretensioned seven-wire strand (Cl 25.4.8)

![[aci318-eq-25.4.8.1-pretensioned-strand-development.png]]
*Eq. 25.4.8.1 — `ℓ_d = (f_se/21)d_b + ((f_ps − f_se)/7)d_b` (ACI 318M-19).*

`[code]` `ℓ_d` is doubled if bonding stops short of the member end and design includes service
tension in the precompressed tension zone (Cl 25.4.8.1(b)). Strand is bonded ≥ `ℓ_d` beyond the
critical section, except that a shorter embedment is allowed where design stress follows the
bilinear relationship of Eq. 25.4.8.1 (Cl 25.4.8.2–25.4.8.3).

![[aci318-figure-R25.4.8.3-strand-bilinear-stress.png]]
*Fig. R25.4.8.3 — idealised bilinear steel-stress / distance-from-free-end relationship (ACI 318M-19 commentary).*

### Deformed bars and wires in compression (Cl 25.4.9)

`[code]` `ℓ_dc` ≥ greater of 200 mm and the larger of the two expressions in Cl 25.4.9.2:

![[aci318-eq-25.4.9.2-compression-development-length.png]]
*Cl 25.4.9.2 — (a) `0.24f_yψ_r d_b/(λ√f'c)`; (b) `0.043 f_y ψ_r d_b` (ACI 318M-19).*

![[aci318-table-25.4.9.3-modification-factors-compression.png]]
*Table 25.4.9.3 — λ (0.75/1.0); ψ_r = 0.75 where the bars are within a spiral, a continuously
wound circular tie (`d_b ≥ 6 mm`, pitch ≤ 100 mm), No. 13/MD130 ties at ≤ 100 mm, or hoops at
≤ 100 mm; otherwise 1.0 (and ψ_r may always be taken as 1.0) (ACI 318M-19).*

### Reduction for excess reinforcement (Cl 25.4.10)

`[code]` Tension, compression and welded-wire development lengths may be reduced by
`A_s,required/A_s,provided` but not below the stated minimums (Cl 25.4.10.1). Not permitted:
at noncontinuous supports; where `f_y` development is required; where bars must be
continuous; for hooked, headed or mechanically anchored bars; in seismic-force-resisting
systems in SDC C–F; and for anchorage of piles to pile caps in SDC C–F (Cl 25.4.10.2).
