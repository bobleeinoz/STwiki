# ACI 318M-19 Appendix B — ASTM reinforcement sizes, areas and masses

> Raw lookup tables reproduced as images from ACI 318M-19 Appendix B (a data page, so it is filed in
> `references/` per CLAUDE.md). **ACI 318M-19 page, kept separate from the AS 3600:2018 pages**
> (see [[aci-318m-19-building-code-concrete]]). Bar sizes here are the **ACI/ASTM metric designations**
> (No. 10 – No. 57, wire MW/MD sizes), not AS/NZS 4671 bar sizes.

## Summary

`[code]` Appendix B tabulates sizes, areas and masses of ASTM standard reinforcement as an aid to users
of the Code. Its use in design is as a lookup for `A_b`, `d_b` and mass; the grades, design strengths and
limits for these bars are in [[aci318-reinforcement-properties-durability-and-embedments]] (Chapter 20)
and their detailing in [[aci318-reinforcement-spacing-hooks-and-development]].

## Detail

### ASTM standard reinforcing bars

![[aci318-table-B-astm-standard-reinforcing-bars.png]]
*Nominal diameter, area and mass for bar Nos. 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25, 29, 32, 36, 43, 57. The bar number
approximates the nominal diameter in millimetres (ACI 318M-19 Appendix B).*

### ASTM standard prestressing strands, wires and bars

![[aci318-table-B-astm-standard-prestressing-strands-wires-bars.png]]
*Seven-wire strand (Grade 1725 and 1860), prestressing wire, Type I plain bars and Type II deformed bars —
nominal diameter, area and mass. Availability of some tendon sizes should be checked in advance
(ACI 318M-19 Appendix B).*

### ASTM standard wire reinforcement

![[aci318-table-B-astm-standard-wire-reinforcement.png]]
*Plain (MW) and deformed (MD) wire sizes MW/MD25 through MW/MD200 — nominal diameter, mass, and area per metre
width at 50–300 mm centre-to-centre spacing (ACI 318M-19 Appendix B).*
