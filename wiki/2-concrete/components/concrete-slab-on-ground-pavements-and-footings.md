# Slab-on-ground floors, pavements and footings

> Scope: AS 3600 Section 21 in full — general applicability (Cl 21.1),
> design considerations (Cl 21.2), and footings — reinforced two-way
> footings and plain concrete footings (Cl 21.3). No figures in this
> section.

## Summary

`[code]` Section 21 sets **additional** design considerations for slab-on-
ground floors, pavements and footings — including plain concrete pavements
and SFRC meeting Cl 16.6 (fire) — but **excludes** residential floors and
pavements designed to AS 2870 (Cl 21.1).

## Detail

### Design considerations (Cl 21.2)

`[code]` Design of ground-supported pavements/slabs and any joints in them
must address, without being limited to: design-load determination;
soil-structure interaction; the pavement/slab's influence on the rest of the
structure's behaviour; effects of traffic on joints; differential movement
at joints; limiting moisture passage through the slab/pavement; water-
pressure effects; and techniques to control/minimise cracking, shrinkage
warping, and differential temperature effects.

### Reinforced footings (Cl 21.3.1)

`[code]` For two-way footings: (a) minimum flexural reinforcement is
designed per Section 9 or Section 16 (see
[[concrete-slab-strength-in-bending]] or
[[concrete-sfrc-member-design]]); (b) minimum tensile reinforcement ratio
`ρmin = 0.19(D/d)² · f'ctf/fsy`; (c) for fibre-reinforced concrete, minimum
fibre dosage conforms with Cl 16.3.3.8 (see
[[concrete-sfrc-classification-and-tensile-properties]]).

### Plain concrete footings (Cl 21.3.2)

`[code]` Plain concrete footings conform with Cl 20.4 in full — see
[[concrete-plain-pedestals-and-footings]].
