# ACI 318M-19 construction documents, concrete materials, mixtures and production

> Scope: ACI 318M-19 Cl 26.1–26.5. Chapter 26 splits every topic into **design information**
> (what the licensed design professional puts in the construction documents) and
> **compliance requirements** (what the contractor/producer must do). Reinforcement, anchors,
> embedments, precast, prestressing and formwork are in
> [[aci318-reinforcement-anchors-embedments-precast-and-formwork-requirements]]; testing,
> acceptance and inspection in [[aci318-concrete-acceptance-testing-and-inspection]].
> **ACI 318M-19 page, kept separate from the AS 3600:2018 pages** (see
> [[aci-318m-19-building-code-concrete]]); materials/durability context:
> [[aci318-concrete-design-properties-and-durability]].

## Summary

`[code]` The chapter states what the design professional must specify (Cl 26.1.1(a)),
what compliance is required (Cl 26.1.1(b)) and what inspection is required (Cl 26.1.1(c)).
Each section below lists the *design information* (D) and *compliance* (C) items.

## Detail

### Design criteria (Cl 26.2)

`[code]` D: name and year of the Code, general building code and supplements; design loads;
design work delegated to the contractor with its criteria (Cl 26.2.1). C: delegated design is
performed by a specialty engineer, compatible with the construction documents and criteria, and
the contractor submits information proving compliance (Cl 26.2.2).

### Member information (Cl 26.3)

`[code]` D: member size, location and tolerances; members built with shotcrete; members for which
modulus-of-elasticity testing of mixtures is required (Cl 26.3.1). C: shotcrete in members not
identified as shotcrete members is permitted only per the project contract documents (Cl 26.3.2).

### Cementitious materials, aggregates, water and admixtures (Cl 26.4.1)

![[aci318-table-26.4.1.1.1a-cementitious-material-specifications.png]]
*Table 26.4.1.1.1(a) — cementitious materials: portland cement ASTM C150; blended cement ASTM C595
(excluding Type IS ≥ 70 and IT with S ≥ 70); expansive cement C845; hydraulic cement C1157; fly ash
and natural pozzolan C618; slag cement C989; silica fume C1240 (ACI 318M-19).* Alternative cements
need approval by the licensed design professional and building official on test data covering
structural, fire and durability performance (Cl 26.4.1.1.1(b)).

`[code]` **Aggregates:** normalweight ASTM C33, lightweight ASTM C330; others allowed if shown by test or
service record to give adequate strength/durability and approved by the building official; crushed
hydraulic-cement concrete or recycled aggregate only with approval backed by demonstrated mechanical
properties/durability and a testing/QC programme throughout the Work; shotcrete gradation ASTM C1436
(Cl 26.4.1.2). Mineral fillers ASTM C1797 (Cl 26.4.1.3); mixing water ASTM C1602 (Cl 26.4.1.4).
**Admixtures:** water reducers/set modifiers ASTM C494; flowing concrete C1017; air entrainment C260;
corrosion inhibitors C1582; others need prior review; admixtures in expansive-cement concrete must be
compatible; shotcrete admixtures ASTM C1141 (Cl 26.4.1.5). **Steel fibre** for shear: deformed, ASTM
A820, length/diameter 50–100 (Cl 26.4.1.6). Dry packaged shotcrete material ASTM C1480 (Cl 26.4.1.7).

### Mixture requirements (Cl 26.4.2)

`[code]` D — per mixture (Cl 26.4.2.1(a)): `f'c`; minimum `E_c` if specified (Cl 19.2.2.2); test age if
not 28 days; maximum `w/cm` for the most restrictive exposure class (Table 19.3.2.1); nominal maximum
coarse-aggregate size not exceeding the least of one-fifth the narrowest form dimension, one-third slab
depth, and three-quarters of the minimum clear spacing between bars/tendons/ducts (waived if workability
and consolidation permit placing without voids); air content for exposure category F; F3 limits on
supplementary cementitious materials; prohibition of carbonate-derived mineral fillers for S1–S3 unless
approved; cementitious materials for category S and whether alternatives per Cl 26.4.2.2(c) are
permitted; where calcium chloride is prohibited (S2/S3); alkali–aggregate reactivity evaluation for
W1/W2; chloride limits for category C; equilibrium density of lightweight concrete and, if
Table 19.2.4.1(b) is used for λ, submittal of aggregate volume fractions; steel-fibre requirements;
shotcrete maximum aggregate size 13 mm. Also the required strength at construction stages
(Cl 26.4.2.1(c)).

`[code]` C (Cl 26.4.2.2): strengths at construction stages for parts not designed by the licensed
design professional are submitted for review. Concrete in F3 members observes the supplementary
cementitious material limits:

![[aci318-table-26.4.2.2b-scm-limits-exposure-class-f3.png]]
*Table 26.4.2.2(b) — maximum % of total cementitious by mass: fly ash/natural pozzolan 25; slag
cement 50; silica fume 10; fly ash + silica fume 35; fly ash + slag + silica fume 50; SCMs in blended
cements count towards the limits (ACI 318M-19).*

For sulfate exposure, alternative cement combinations are acceptable if ASTM C1012 length change meets:

![[aci318-table-26.4.2.2c-sulfate-resistance-test-criteria.png]]
*Table 26.4.2.2(c) — max expansion %: S1 0.10 at 6 months; S2 0.05 at 6 months (0.10 at 12 months
only if the 6-month limit is exceeded); S3 Option 1 0.10 at 18 months; S3 Option 2 0.05 at 6 months
(0.10 at 12 months if exceeded) (ACI 318M-19).*

`[code]` Concrete exposed to water shows non-reactive aggregates (alkali–silica mitigated; no
alkali–carbonate reactivity) (Cl 26.4.2.2(d)). Chloride limits are demonstrated by calculation from
measured material chlorides or by ASTM C1218 on hardened concrete at 28–42 days (Cl 26.4.2.2(e)). No
calcium-chloride admixtures in prestressed concrete (Cl 26.4.2.2(f)); concrete against stay-in-place
galvanized forms ≤ 0.30 % water-soluble chloride (Cl 26.4.2.2(g)). Lightweight concrete fresh density
(ASTM C138) corresponds to the specified equilibrium density (ASTM C567) and is the acceptance basis
(Cl 26.4.2.2(h)). Steel-fibre concrete for shear: ASTM C1116 and ≥ 60 kg/m³ deformed fibres
(Cl 26.4.2.2(i)).

### Proportioning and documentation (Cl 26.4.3–26.4.4)

`[code]` Mixtures are placed without segregation, encase reinforcement, meet the specified durability,
meet strength tests of standard-cured specimens, and where `E_c` is specified meet it: average of ≥ 3
laboratory-cured cylinders (ASTM C192/C469) tested at 28 days or the stated age, acceptable if the
measured value ≥ specified (Cl 26.4.3.1(a)). Proportions follow ACI 301M Art. 4.2.3 (strength records
≤ 24 months old) or an alternative with at least equal probability of passing acceptance tests
(Cl 26.4.3.1(b)); materials used for proportioning match those on the Work, and each mixture meets
its stated requirements (Cl 26.4.3.1(c)–(d)); shotcrete proportioning follows Cl 26.4.3.1(e).
Mixture documentation is submitted before use and before changes, with consecutive strength-test records
or laboratory trial batches; without test data, `f'c ≤ 35 MPa` mixtures may rely on approved experience,
while `f'c > 35 MPa` requires test data (Cl 26.4.4.1).

### Production, placement, curing (Cl 26.5.1–26.5.3)

`[code]` Materials are stored free of deterioration/contamination; equipment and batching per ASTM C94
or C685 (Cl 26.5.1.1). Placement: remove debris/ice and standing water (tremie excepted); no pumping
through aluminium pipe; place at a rate maintaining workability, without segregation or cold joints;
contaminated or unworkable concrete is not used; retempering per ASTM C94 unless restricted; continuous
placement to joint boundaries; consolidate around reinforcement and embedments (Cl 26.5.2.1(a)–(i)).
Shotcrete: remove rebound/overspray, rough surfaces to about 6 mm amplitude before final set, remove
laitance and dampen before adding material, replace defective shotcrete, certified nozzle operators who
have passed a mock-up when required (Cl 26.5.2.1(j)–(p)).

`[code]` **Curing.** Concrete other than high-early-strength is kept at ≥ **10 °C** and moist for the first
**7 days**; high-early-strength for **3 days**, unless accelerated curing is used (steam, heat and
moisture, etc.), in which case strength at each load stage is at least that required and durability is not
impaired (Cl 26.5.3.2(a)–(c)). Field-cured companion cylinders (≥ 2 of 150×300 mm or ≥ 3 of 100×200 mm,
ASTM C31/C39) may be required; curing is adequate if their average is ≥ 85 % of companion standard-cured
cylinders or exceeds `f'c` by more than 3.5 MPa (Cl 26.5.3.2(d)–(e)). Shotcrete: 24 h initial curing
(ponding/fogging, moist mat, or membrane) then final curing for 7 days (3 days with high-early cement or
accelerator) (Cl 26.5.3.2(f)). D: number/size/frequency of supplementary field-cured tests if required
(Cl 26.5.3.1).

### Cold and hot weather, joints, member construction (Cl 26.5.4–26.5.7)

`[code]` D: delivered-concrete temperature limits for cold weather (Cl 26.5.4.1) and hot weather
(Cl 26.5.5.1). C: heating/protection equipment, no frozen or iced materials, forms and ground free of
frost, delivery temperature within limits (Cl 26.5.4.2); in hot weather, temperature and evaporation are
controlled to avoid strength, serviceability and durability loss (Cl 26.5.5.2).

`[code]` **Joints (Cl 26.5.6).** D: locations and details of construction, isolation and contraction joints;
shear-transfer details; surface preparation and roughening; clean steel surfaces where studs or welded
bars transfer shear; composite topping preparation; shotcrete square-joint locations (Cl 26.5.6.1).
C: unshown or changed joints are submitted for review; outside prestressed concrete, floor/roof
construction joints lie within the **middle third** of spans of slabs, beams and girders; girder joints
are offset ≥ 2× the width of intersecting beams from the beam face; joints are cleaned of laitance,
roughened if specified, and prewetted with standing water removed; shotcrete joints cut at 45° unless
square joints are designated (Cl 26.5.6.2).

`[code]` **Member construction (Cl 26.5.7).** D: details for dimensional change from prestress, creep,
shrinkage and temperature; whether a slab-on-ground is a structural diaphragm or part of the
seismic-force-resisting system; sloped/stepped footings acting as a unit; floor–column integrated
placement locations (Cl 15.5); steel-fibre shear locations (Cl 26.5.7.1). C: beams/slabs are not cast until
supporting columns/walls are no longer plastic; drop panels, shear caps and capitals are monolithic with
the slab unless shown otherwise; where integrated placement is required, column concrete extends
**600 mm** into the floor system from the column face; no saw-cutting or joints in diaphragm slabs-on-ground
without approval (Cl 26.5.7.2).
