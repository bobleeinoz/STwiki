# ACI 318M-19 construction requirements — reinforcement, anchors, embedments, precast, prestressing, formwork

> Scope: ACI 318M-19 Cl 26.6–26.11, with D = design information the design professional specifies
> and C = compliance requirement. Concrete materials, curing and joints are in
> [[aci318-construction-documents-concrete-materials-and-production]]; testing and inspection in
> [[aci318-concrete-acceptance-testing-and-inspection]]. **ACI 318M-19 page, kept separate from the
> AS 3600:2018 pages** (see [[aci-318m-19-building-code-concrete]]).

## Summary

`[code]` Specification items and workmanship rules for reinforcement (placement tolerances, bending,
welding), anchors, embedments, precast, prestressed members and formwork.

## Detail

### Reinforcement — general and placement (Cl 26.6.1–26.6.2)

`[code]` D (Cl 26.6.1.1): ASTM designation and grade including seismic requirements (Cl 20.2.2.5); type,
size, location, detailing and embedment; cover; location/length of lap splices; type and location of
mechanical, end-bearing and welded splices and other welding; protective-coating ASTM designation;
corrosion protection for exposed bars for future work. C (Cl 26.6.1.2): mill test reports submitted;
rust/mill scale acceptable if a hand-wire-brushed specimen still meets ASTM dimensions (including
deformation height) and mass per length; prestressing steel free of mill scale, pitting and excessive rust
(light rust allowed); bonded reinforcement clean of ice, mud, oil and bond-reducing coatings at placing.

`[code]` D — tolerances (Cl 26.6.2.1): on `d` and cover per Table 26.6.2.1(a); on longitudinal location of bends
and ends per Table 26.6.2.1(b); the Table 26.6.2.1(a) cover tolerance also applies at discontinuous member
ends. Hoop spacing in intermediate and special seismic systems: lesser of +40 mm and +1.5× the smallest
longitudinal bar diameter, and lesser of 1/12 the least member side and −75 mm, with no more than two hoops
touching after adjustment.

![[aci318-table-26.6.2.1a-tolerances-on-d-and-cover.png]]
*Table 26.6.2.1(a) — `d ≤ 200 mm`: tolerance on `d` ±10 mm; `d > 200 mm`: ±13 mm; specified cover
tolerance is the smaller of −10 mm (−13 mm for `d > 200`) and −(1/3) specified cover; cover to formed
soffits −6 mm (ACI 318M-19).*

![[aci318-table-26.6.2.1b-tolerances-bend-and-end-locations.png]]
*Table 26.6.2.1(b) — longitudinal location of bends/ends: ±13 mm at discontinuous ends of brackets and
corbels, ±25 mm at discontinuous ends of other members, ±50 mm elsewhere (ACI 318M-19).*

`[code]` C (Cl 26.6.2.2): reinforcement, including bundles, is placed within tolerance and supported against
displacement; spirals are continuous and evenly spaced; splices only as permitted or authorised;
end-bearing splices keep square-cut ends in concentric contact, ends within 1.5° of square and bearing
within 3°.

### Bending and welding (Cl 26.6.3–26.6.4)

`[code]` D: nonstandard bend geometry (Cl 26.6.3.1). C: bars are bent cold before placing unless permitted;
no field bending of partly embedded bars except as shown or permitted; offset bars bent before placing
(Cl 26.6.3.2). Welding: D — details for anchor-bar welds at the front face of brackets/corbels
(Cl 26.6.4.1). C — welding of nonprestressed bars per **AWS D1.4**; ASTM bar specifications other than A706 are
supplemented by a mill test report demonstrating AWS D1.4 conformance; welding crossing bars to assemble
reinforcement is not allowed except at bracket/corbel front faces or as permitted (Cl 26.6.4.2).

### Anchoring to concrete (Cl 26.7)

`[code]` D (Cl 26.7.1): anchor assessment/qualification per Cl 17.1.2; type, size, location, effective
embedment and installation; anchor reinforcement and column/pedestal confinement (Cl 17.5.2.1, 10.7.6.1.5);
shear lugs (Cl 17.11) and base-plate inspection/vent holes; minimum edge distance (Cl 17.9); corrosion
protection of exposed anchors; for post-installed anchors the strength parameters of Cl 17.5 (category,
concrete strength, aggregate and lightweight type, torque, hole preparation); for adhesive anchors the bond
stress parameters of Cl 17.6.5, identification of horizontal/upward anchors resisting sustained tension and of
anchors needing proof load (ACI 355.4M), and installer certification. C (Cl 26.7.2): cast-in anchors securely
positioned with concrete consolidated around them; consolidation at shear lugs verified through inspection
holes; post-installed anchors installed per manufacturer instructions (MPII for adhesives) by qualified
installers, with certified installers for horizontal/upward sustained-tension adhesive anchors; adhesive
anchors installed in concrete at least **21 days** old. Design: [[aci318-anchoring-general-and-tensile-strength]],
[[aci318-anchoring-shear-interaction-seismic-and-shear-lugs]].

### Embedments (Cl 26.8)

`[code]` D: type, size, details and location of designed embedments; steel perpendicular to pipes; pipe cover;
corrosion protection for exposed embedments (Cl 26.8.1). C: unshown embedments submitted for review;
aluminium coated/covered; pipes designed for their contents' material, pressure and temperature; nothing
except water ≤ 32 °C or 0.35 MPa goes in embedded pipes before the concrete reaches specified strength;
in solid slabs, piping (except radiant heating/snow melting) lies between top and bottom reinforcement;
conduit and pipes must not require cutting, bending or displacing reinforcement (Cl 26.8.2). Design limits:
[[aci318-reinforcement-properties-durability-and-embedments]] (Cl 20.6).

### Precast concrete (Cl 26.9)

`[code]` D: dimensional tolerances for members and interfaces; lifting devices, embedments and related
reinforcement for handling/storage/transport/erection if designed by the design professional (Cl 26.9.1).
C: members marked for location, orientation and date of manufacture, matching erection drawings; lifting
design provided if not by the design professional; members braced and supported during erection until
permanent connections are complete; plastic-state embedded items (if approved) protrude or stay exposed, need
not be hooked/tied, are held in position and consolidated around (Cl 26.9.2). Design: [[aci318-connections-between-members]].

### Prestressed concrete (Cl 26.10)

`[code]` D (Cl 26.10.1): prestressing force magnitude and location; stressing sequence; post-tensioning
anchorage type, size, details and location; tendon/duct tolerances per Table 26.6.2.1(a); corrosion-protection
materials and details; duct requirements; grouting requirements including chloride limit (Cl 19.4.1).
C (Cl 26.10.2): unshown anchorage systems submitted; tendons and ducts placed within tolerance and supported;
couplers placed where approved and housed for movement; burning/welding near tendons controlled to avoid
sparks, ground currents and damaging temperatures; force and friction loss verified by **both** measured
elongation (vs calculated using tested or manufacturer `E_p`) **and** calibrated jacking force; any difference
over **5 %** (pretensioned) or **7 %** (post-tensioned) is investigated and corrected unless approved;
unreplaced broken tendons ≤ **2 %** of the total prestress force; flame-cutting location and sequence chosen to
avoid undesired temporary stresses; long exposed pretensioned strand is cut near the member. Post-tensioning
is not applied until concrete reaches ≥ **17 MPa** (single-strand or bar tendons) or ≥ **28 MPa** (multistrand
tendons), or more if required; lower strengths are permitted with oversized anchorage devices or if tendons
are stressed to no more than 50 % of the final force (Cl 26.10.2(j)–(k)). Related:
[[aci318-post-tensioning-anchorages-and-anchorage-zones]].

### Formwork (Cl 26.11)

`[code]` D: contractor designs, fabricates, installs and removes formwork; composite members requiring
shoring and shoring-removal requirements (Cl 26.11.1.1). C — design considers placement method and rate,
construction loads (vertical, horizontal, impact), avoiding damage to existing members, and for
post-tensioned members movement and load redistribution on formwork during tensioning; formwork gives
required shapes and dimensions, is tight against paste/mortar leakage, and is braced (Cl 26.11.1.2).
Removal: contractor develops a removal and reshoring procedure and schedule and calculates transferred
loads; analysis and strength requirements are furnished to the design professional and building official
on request; no construction load or formwork removal unless the structure with remaining formwork can
safely carry its weight and loads without impairing serviceability, demonstrated by analysis using
anticipated loads, formwork strength and an in-place strength estimate (from field-cured cylinders or
other approved procedures); formwork removal must not damage concrete; post-tensioned member supports
stay until enough prestress carries dead load and construction loads; no construction loads beyond
superimposed dead plus reduced live load on unshored parts without analysis (Cl 26.11.2.1).
