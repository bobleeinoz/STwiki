# Construction joints, tolerances and formwork

> Scope: AS 3600 Cl 17.4 (construction joints and unshown embedded items),
> Cl 17.5 (position/size tolerances for structures, members, reinforcement
> and tendons), Cl 17.6 (formwork and stripping/removal timing).

## Summary

`[code]` Construction joints (Cl 17.4.1) are located per Cl 19.1.2 to
facilitate placing per Cl 17.1.3; where a placement interruption breaches Cl
17.1.3(c)/(d)/(e), a Cl 19.1.2-conforming joint is made at an appropriate
location. Unshown embedded items/driven fixings/holes (Cl 17.4.2) are
located so member behaviour is not impaired (Cl 19.2).

## Detail

### Tolerances (Cl 17.5)

`[code]` For strength purposes, any surface point's position conforms with
Cl 17.5.2 (Cl 17.5.1) — more stringent serviceability/fit/aesthetic
tolerances may separately apply. AS 3610 formed-surface tolerances take
precedence unless Cl 17.5.2 is stricter; unformed plane-surface flatness
tolerances are project-specified, not exceeding Cl 17.5.2.

`[code]` **Position/size** (Cl 17.5.2): plan deviation for a column/wall
surface point — 40 mm (first 20 storeys), +15 mm per additional 10 storeys;
elevation deviation at a floor-top/soffit adjacent to a column/wall — 40 mm.
Floor-to-floor plumb deviation ≤ the greater of `1/200` × storey height or
10 mm. Deviation from specified height/plan/cross-section dimension ≤ the
greater of `1/200` × dimension or 5 mm. Surface-alignment deviation from a
straight line between two points ≤ `1/250` × line length.

`[code]` **Reinforcement/tendon position** (Cl 17.5.3): cover-controlled
positions — beams/slabs/columns/walls −5/+10 mm; slabs-on-ground −10/+20 mm;
footings cast in ground −10/+40 mm (positive = cover increase). Non-cover-
controlled: tendon-profile location ±5 mm; reinforcement end position
±50 mm; bar/fitment spacing ± the greater of 10% of specified spacing or
15 mm.

### Formwork and stripping (Cl 17.6)

`[code]` Formwork materials/design/construction conform with AS 3610;
stripping/support-removal follows Cl 17.6.2 where stricter (Cl 17.6.1).

`[code]` **General stripping rules** (Cl 17.6.2.1): no stripping/support
removal until the unsupported part has attained Section 2 strength to carry
its own weight and concurrent/subsequent construction loads safely, without
detriment; removal is sequenced to avoid unnecessary deformation/impact/
eccentric loading; vertical-surface removal per Cl 17.6.2.2; soffit
stripping between supports per Cl 17.6.2.3 or 17.6.2.4 (backpropping per AS
3610); support removal per Cl 17.6.2.5 (no structure above) or 17.6.2.6
(multistorey); prestressed soffits per Cl 17.6.2.7; surfaces exposed early
are cured to the end of the specified curing period.

`[code]` Vertical surfaces (Cl 17.6.2.2): not removed until Cl 3.1.1.2
strength resists surface damage; extra care if stripped <18 hours after
casting.

`[code]` Soffit stripping with control samples (Cl 17.6.2.3): permitted
between formwork supports if elapsed time >3 days **and** remaining spans
stay uncracked under bending/shear from maximum concurrent/subsequent
construction loads — design resistance taken as `Ru` (Cl 2.2), mean in-situ
strength from control-sample averages (Cl 3.1.1.2).

`[code]` Reinforced normal-class slabs, no control samples (Cl 17.6.2.4,
Table 17.6.2.4): minimum period by average ambient temperature over the
period, increased for `Ls/D ≥ (280 − D)/100`, superimposed load >2.0 kPa, or
cold weather (½ day per day at 2–5°C average, 1 day per day below 2°C).

![[as3600-table-17.6.2.4-stripping-formwork-continuous-slabs.png]]
*Table 17.6.2.4 — stripping time for formwork from reinforced slabs
continuous over formwork supports (AS 3600:2018).*

| Average temperature `T` (°C) | Period before stripping (days) |
| --- | --- |
| `T > 20` | 4 |
| `T > 12` | 6 |
| `T > 5` | 8 |

`[code]` Support removal, no structure above, no early-age data (Cl
17.6.2.5, Table 17.6.2.5), same temperature/load increase rules as above:

![[as3600-table-17.6.2.5-removal-formwork-supports.png]]
*Table 17.6.2.5 — removal of formwork supports from slabs and beams not
supporting structures above (AS 3600:2018).*

| Average temperature `T` (°C) | Period before support removal (days) |
| --- | --- |
| `T > 20` | 12 |
| `T > 12` | 18 |
| `T > 5` | 24 |

`[code]` Multistorey structures (Cl 17.6.2.6): the number of storeys kept
formwork-supported, and support spacing, is calculated from each floor's
concrete properties and the formwork/structure interaction at that time; if
removal leaves floors above supported mainly by formwork/supported
construction, all affected floors/beams are checked by calculation for
cracking/deflection, and removal proceeds only if results stay within
strength/serviceability limits. No undisturbed support/backprop is removed
within 2 days of placing any slab it supports.

`[code]` Prestressed soffits (Cl 17.6.2.7): not stripped/unsupported until
concrete strength and stressed-tendon count give the necessary strength for
permanent + construction-load actions and associated serviceability limits.

`[code]` Control tests (Cl 17.6.2.8): where formwork removal or tendon
stressing is planned before design strength is reached, control samples are
taken at ≥1 per 50 m³ (or part) of a grade placed per day, cured under
job-like conditions; ≥2 specimens per grade are tested at the desired
stripping/stressing age, with strength assessed from the average.
