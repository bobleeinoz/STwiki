# Selection of corrosion protection requirements

> Scope: AS 4100:2020 Appendix C (informative) — pointer-level guidance for
> selecting a corrosion protection system for steel members and connection
> components: scope, selection factors, classification of environments
> (AS 4312 corrosivity categories), and protection systems (paint per
> AS 2312.1, hot-dip galvanizing per AS/NZS 2312.2). Referenced from
> Cl 3.5.6 (serviceability limit state — corrosion protection).

## Summary

`[code]` AS 4100 itself sets **no numeric corrosion-protection
requirement** — Cl 3.5.6 requires protection "based on the use of the
structure, its maintenance, and the climatic or other local conditions,"
and this appendix points to the governing companion Standards: **AS 4312**
for atmospheric corrosivity classification, **AS 2312.1** for paint
systems, **AS/NZS 2312.2** for hot-dip galvanizing.

## Detail

### Scope and selection factors (Cl C.1–C.2)

`[code]` Applies to corrosion protection of steel members and connection
components. Selection should account for: (a) the exposure environment;
(b) the structure's shape and detailing; (c) required coating life to
first maintenance, or design life, as appropriate; (d) cost-benefit of
available coating systems over the structure's lifetime. AS 2312.1 guides
paint-system selection; AS/NZS 2312.2 guides hot-dip-galvanizing
protection. Further application guidance: Australian Steel Institute (ASI)
Number 870, *Australian steelwork corrosion and coatings guide*.

### Classification of environments (Cl C.3)

`[code]` Corrosion of steel and coating-system life are influenced by both
the **macroclimatic** environment and local **micro-environments** (from
structure detailing or localised exposure). **AS 4312**, based on ISO 9223,
classifies atmospheric corrosivity into categories **C1 to CX**, in order
of increasing corrosion rate. AS 2312.1 and AS/NZS 2312.2 define paint-
system and galvanizing durability respectively against these categories.
AS 4312, AS 2312.1 and AS/NZS 2312.2 all provide guidance on
micro-environmental factors.

### Protection systems (Cl C.4)

`[code]` **AS 2312.1** (paint): paint systems are described by a
standardised "system designation" that includes surface-preparation
requirements; durability (years to first maintenance) is tabulated by
system designation and corrosivity category. **AS/NZS 2312.2** (hot-dip
galvanizing): durability class and expected life are defined for each
corrosivity category and galvanizing type.

`[derived]` Cl 3.5.6 Note 4 (see [[steel-limit-state-design-basis]]) adds
the operative rule this appendix supports: **no corrosion allowance is
needed** in corrosivity categories C1/C2 with a coating to AS 2312.1 or
hot-dip galvanizing to AS/NZS 4680 (with AS/NZS 2312.2 detailing); for C3–
CX, the coating system and maintenance strategy should be the subject of
professional advice — i.e. AS 4100 treats corrosion protection as a
durability/maintenance decision outside its own strength-design
provisions, not as a design allowance to be subtracted from section
properties.
