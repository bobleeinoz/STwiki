# Statistical data for brittle fracture material qualification

> Scope: AS 4100:2020 Appendix K (normative) — the test regime for
> generating statistical impact-toughness data to qualify a steel product
> **not** already covered by Table 10.4.1, for use in the Cl 10.2
> notch-ductile-range brittle-fracture method. See
> [[steel-brittle-fracture-material-selection]] for how Table 10.4.1 itself
> is used.

## Summary

`[code]` For a product not covered by Table 10.4.1, generate statistical
impact-test data per this appendix — test to Section 17 and the relevant
product Standard (AS/NZS 1163, 3678, 3679.1 or 3679.2), minimum test
numbers per that Standard's initial-type-testing regime, all from one
factory-production-controlled facility, absorbed energy assessed against
Table K.4.

## Detail

### Scope and statistical data (Cl K.1–K.2)

`[code]` Sets out test requirements to produce the statistical data needed
for products not covered by Table 10.4.1 (K.1). Impact-test statistical
data must be assessed to ensure products meet Table 10.4.1 requirements
(K.2):
- Cold-formed structural hollow sections: test per **AS/NZS 1163**.
- Hot-rolled plate and hot-rolled flat products: test per **AS/NZS 3678**.
- Hot-rolled bars and sections: test per **AS/NZS 3679.1**.

### Testing (Cl K.3)

`[code]` General (K.3.1): testing per Section 17 and, as applicable,
AS/NZS 1163, AS/NZS 3678, AS/NZS 3679.1, AS/NZS 3679.2.

`[code]` Minimum number of tests (K.3.2): per the **initial type testing**
requirements for impact grades in Appendix B of the relevant product
Standard (AS/NZS 1163, 3678, 3679.1, 3679.2). All tests from the **same
facility**, with factory production control per that product Standard.

### Mechanical properties (Cl K.4, Table K.4)

`[code]` Absorbed energy values (joules), for all tests at the Table 10.4.1
temperatures, must conform to Table K.4. Retests only per the retest
requirements in the relevant product Standard.

`[code]` Table K.4 — Charpy V-notch impact test requirements, minimum
absorbed energy:

| Test piece size | Average of 3 tests | Individual test |
|---|---|---|
| 10 × 10 mm | 27 J | 20 J |
| 10 × 7.5 mm | 22 J | 16 J |
| 10 × 5 mm | 18 J | 13 J |

`[derived]` These are the same absorbed-energy values that appear directly
in Cl 10.4.3.4 (non-conforming conditions: 27 J average / 20 J individual
for a 10×10 mm piece, scaled down for smaller test pieces where full-size
specimens don't fit the material thickness) — Appendix K is the systematic,
factory-qualification version of the same acceptance criterion that
Cl 10.4.3.4 applies as a one-off mock-up test.
